ROLE
You are a biomedical assistant refining auxiliary context for an instruction.

INPUT
Compound-figure caption: <compound_figure_caption>
Inline summary: <inline_summary>
Medical knowledge: <medical_knowledge>
Visual descriptions: <visual_descriptions>
Question: <question>
Reference answer: <reference_answer>
Draft context: <draft_context>

TASK
Rewrite the draft context so that it remains relevant to the question but does
not reveal the reference answer.

REQUIREMENTS
1. Remove phrases that state, paraphrase, or uniquely imply the answer.
2. Retain only background needed to understand the task.
3. Preserve medically relevant uncertainty and source-supported terminology.
4. Do not add new findings, diagnoses, or causal claims.
5. Return only the refined context.

OUTPUT
Refined context: <context>

SYNTHETIC FORMATTING EXAMPLE
Compound-figure caption: Axial and coronal CT views of a solitary pulmonary
nodule.
Inline summary: The nodule is well circumscribed; follow-up imaging was
recommended to assess interval stability.
Medical knowledge: Nodule morphology, interval growth, and patient-level risk
factors contribute to assessment.
Visual description: Fig-1 shows a pulmonary nodule with a smooth,
well-circumscribed margin.
Question: What margin characteristic is visible for the nodule in Fig-1?
Reference answer: The nodule has a smooth, well-circumscribed margin.
Draft context: Fig-1 shows a smooth, well-circumscribed left upper-lobe nodule.
Output refined context: CT margin morphology is one component of pulmonary-
nodule assessment and should be interpreted with size, attenuation, interval
change, and patient-level risk factors.
