ROLE
You are a biomedical assistant constructing a text-only QA instruction. The
downstream model will not receive an image.

INPUT
Compound-figure caption: <compound_figure_caption>
Inline summary: <inline_summary>
Medical knowledge: <medical_knowledge>

TASK
Construct a self-contained context-question-answer instruction from the supplied
textual information.

REQUIREMENTS
1. The question and answer must be understandable without visual access.
2. Do not refer to an image, figure, panel, subimage, or visual location.
3. Use the caption and inline summary to identify the topic; use the medical-
   knowledge note to formulate a related, self-contained question.
4. Keep the context useful but avoid directly revealing the answer.
5. Preserve uncertainty and avoid unsupported patient-specific conclusions.

OUTPUT
Context: <text-only auxiliary context>
Question: <self-contained medical question>
Answer: <reference answer>

SYNTHETIC FORMATTING EXAMPLE
Input caption: Axial and coronal chest CT views of a solitary pulmonary nodule.
Input inline summary: Follow-up imaging was recommended to assess interval
stability.
Input medical knowledge: Pulmonary-nodule assessment integrates size,
attenuation, morphology, interval growth, and patient-level risk factors.
Output context: Pulmonary nodules are assessed using imaging characteristics,
interval change, and patient-level risk factors.
Output question: Which factors are commonly considered when estimating the
risk of malignancy in a pulmonary nodule?
Output answer: Assessment commonly considers nodule size, attenuation,
morphology, interval growth, and patient-level factors such as age, smoking
history, and prior malignancy.
