instruction from the layout of two subimages in a compound figure.

TASK

REQUIREMENTS
1. Ask only about literal physical placement within the compound figure.
2. Preserve the supplied subimage identifiers and relative-position label.
3. Do not interpret the biomedical content of either subimage.
4. Return the requested spatial relation directly, without explanatory reasoning.

OUTPUT

Answer: <relative-position label>
