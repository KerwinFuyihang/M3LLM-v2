ROLE
You are a biomedical assistant constructing a four-option multiple-choice VQA
instruction.

CONSTRUCTION INPUTS
Target visual-input type: <compound figure or multi-image input>
Target visual identifiers: <Fig-0 or selected subimage identifiers>
Compound-figure caption: <compound_figure_caption>
Inline summary: <inline_summary>
Relevant visual descriptions: <visual_descriptions>
Medical knowledge: <medical_knowledge>
Required correct-option position: <A, B, C, or D>

TASK
Construct one clinically or scientifically relevant question with one supported
answer and three plausible distractors.

REQUIREMENTS
1. Make the question answerable from the supplied visual evidence and context.
2. Provide exactly four mutually exclusive options labelled A-D.
3. Include one unambiguously correct option and three plausible but incorrect
   distractors drawn from the same conceptual domain.
4. Avoid answer cues based on wording, length, or option position.
5. Place the correct option at the required position supplied by the dataset-
   construction pipeline.

OUTPUT
Question: <question>
Options: A. <option>; B. <option>; C. <option>; D. <option>
Correct answer: <A, B, C, or D>

SYNTHETIC FORMATTING EXAMPLE
Target visual-input type: multi-image input.
Target visual identifiers: Fig-1.
Compound-figure caption: Axial and coronal CT views of a solitary pulmonary
nodule.
Inline summary: The nodule is well circumscribed; follow-up imaging was
recommended to assess interval stability.
Relevant visual description: Fig-1 shows a pulmonary nodule with a smooth,
well-circumscribed margin.
Medical knowledge: Margin morphology is one feature considered in
pulmonary-nodule assessment.
Required correct-option position: B.
Question: Which visible feature in Fig-1 describes the pulmonary nodule margin?
Options: A. Spiculated; B. Smooth and well circumscribed; C. Lobulated;
D. Indistinct
Correct answer: B
